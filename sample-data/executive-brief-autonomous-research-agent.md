# AI Governance Brief: Autonomous Research Agent

## BLUF

The Autonomous Research Agent should remain in testing and should not scale to operational use yet. The system has useful research-synthesis potential, but its truth-verifiability performance, prompt-injection resistance, and shutdown exercise remain incomplete. Approval should be delayed until independent red-team testing and frontier gate reauthorization are complete.

## Why This Matters

The system may influence technical direction, vendor attention, investment priorities, and strategic research decisions. If it overstates weak evidence, it may misdirect resources.

## Main Risk

The highest risk is not ordinary hallucination. The highest risk is confident synthesis of weak or adversarial information into decision-ready language.

## Evidence

Current evidence includes system logs, lab tests, read-only access controls, and initial source-grading rules. Missing evidence includes independent red-team results and tested shutdown procedures.

## Decision Required

Delay scaled deployment.

## Conditions for Approval

Complete red-team testing, truth-verifiability testing, prompt-injection testing, and shutdown-path exercise.
