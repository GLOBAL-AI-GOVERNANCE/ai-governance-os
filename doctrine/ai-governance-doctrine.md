# AI Governance Doctrine

AI governance is not anti-AI. It is the discipline that makes AI useful, trusted, secure, and accountable.

## Core Doctrine

Low-risk AI should move fast.
High-impact AI should move carefully.
Frontier AI should move only through gates.
Unowned AI should not move at all.

## Operating Principles

- Every AI system must be inventoried.
- Every AI system must have a named owner.
- Every AI system must have a risk tier.
- Every high-impact system must have evidence before approval.
- Every frontier system must have capability forecasting, red-teaming, live monitoring, and reauthorization.
- Every incident must be logged, contained, assessed, corrected, and reviewed.
- Every powerful system must have a rollback or shutdown path.

## Decision Standard

Approve AI only when the purpose, evidence, risk, control, owner, trigger, audit path, and consequence are clear.
