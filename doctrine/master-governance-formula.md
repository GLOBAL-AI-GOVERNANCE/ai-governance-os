# Master Governance Formula

Issue -> Evidence -> Risk -> Control -> Owner -> Trigger -> Audit -> Consequence

## Issue

What is the system doing, and why does it matter?

## Evidence

What proves the system is safe enough, useful enough, and accurate enough for this use?

## Risk

What may go wrong, who may be harmed, and how severe would the harm be?

## Control

What prevents, detects, limits, or corrects the harm?

## Owner

Who is accountable for the system and the control?

## Trigger

What event escalates the issue, pauses deployment, or reopens review?

## Audit

What records prove the decision was governed?

## Consequence

What happens when controls fail or evidence is insufficient?
