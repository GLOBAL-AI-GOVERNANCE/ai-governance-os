# Risk Tiering Model

## Low Risk

Low-impact internal support such as drafting, summarizing, formatting, brainstorming, and basic knowledge assistance.

Required controls:
- Basic user training.
- Data-use limits.
- Owner assigned.
- Simple system card.

## Medium Risk

Operational tools that affect work quality, customer interaction, software development, analytics, or business processes.

Required controls:
- Owner assigned.
- System card.
- Risk assessment.
- Human review for important outputs.
- Monitoring plan.

## High Risk

Systems used in employment, education, healthcare, finance, legal, cybersecurity, critical infrastructure, public benefits, national security, or other high-impact domains.

Required controls:
- Formal risk assessment.
- Approval gate.
- Meaningful human review.
- Testing evidence.
- Logs and monitoring.
- Incident response path.
- Scheduled reauthorization.

## Frontier Risk

Advanced models, autonomous agents, cyber-capable systems, bio-relevant systems, strategic research systems, military-relevant systems, or systems with unclear emergent capability.

Required controls:
- Full frontier gate review.
- Capability forecasting.
- Truth and verifiability review.
- Independent red-team testing.
- Staged deployment.
- Live monitoring.
- Tested shutdown path.
- Reauthorization after major capability change.
