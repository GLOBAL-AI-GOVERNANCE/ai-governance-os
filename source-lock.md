Official source:

- https://www.nist.gov/caisi

## European Union AI rules

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 establishes harmonised rules on artificial intelligence and entered into force on 1 August 2024.

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the Digital Omnibus on AI, is an in-force regulation dated 8 July 2026 and published in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026. It sets 2 December 2027 as the application date for Chapter III, Sections 1, 2, and 3 for high-risk AI systems under Article 6(2) and Annex III, and 2 August 2028 for high-risk AI systems under Article 6(1) and Annex I.

Official sources:

- AI Act: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng
- Digital Omnibus on AI: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj/eng

### Proposed third-party validation and shutdown capability

Official record identifies the proposal as Int. 1100-2026 after introduction on 8 October 2026. It concerns third-party validation, human-operable shutdown capability, validator disclosure of interests, deployment-readiness findings, rulemaking, and civil penalties.

Official source:

- https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/LegislationDetail.aspx?GUID=A927E066-C669-4F66-8E5A-4B610FA321E5&ID=8239233&Options=&Search=

This design uses those ideas as a policy case study. It does not claim that the proposal is enacted law.

### Proposed whistleblower protections

Official record identifies a companion proposal concerning protections for certain City and contractor personnel reporting artificial-intelligence-related conduct they know or reasonably believe presents a substantial and specific public-health or public-safety risk.

This repository must never invite confidential material into public Issues, pull requests, fixtures, or examples.

## Evidence boundary

These sources support general public framing only. They do not establish that an organization or AI system complies with law, operates controls effectively, is safe, or is authorized for deployment. Verify current law, standards, policies, and institutional requirements before consequential use.
